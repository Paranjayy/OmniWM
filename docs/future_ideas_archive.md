# OmniWM Future Roadmap (v31+ Brainstorm)

Here are some next-level ideas to make your workspace even more powerful.

## 1. The "Launcher" Layer (`RCmd + L`)
Instead of searching, you just tap `RCmd + L` followed by a single key to jump straight to your daily drivers:
*   `L -> G`: Ghostty
*   `L -> A`: Arc Browser
*   `L -> Z`: Zed Editor
*   `L -> S`: Spotify

## 2. Picture-in-Picture Mode (`RCmd + P`)
A macro that instantly:
1.  Floats the current window.
2.  Resizes it to a small thumbnail.
3.  Moves it to the bottom-right corner.
*(Perfect for watching a video/stream while working).*

## 3. The "Monitor Throw" (`RCmd + Shift + M`)
Instantly teleport the focused window to the next monitor without having to drag it or move focus manually.

## 4. "Focus Mode" Timer (`RCmd + T`)
A macro that engages **Zen Mode** and starts a 25-minute Pomodoro timer in your menu bar or Workspace Bar.

## 5. Window Swapping (`RCmd + X`)
Instantly swap the focused window with the one to its left/right. Great for quickly rearranging your logical flow without reording the entire bar.

## 6. Smart "Tailing"
A hotkey that snaps a window to the far right/left at exactly 20% width (perfect for keeping documentation or chat visible while coding at 80% width).
